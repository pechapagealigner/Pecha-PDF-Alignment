Pecha PDF Alignment

Upload your PDF and arrange the pages automatically.

Choose alignment

Pages are divided into consecutive parts while keeping duplex page-pairs together. Front positions are normal and back positions are reversed. Automatic blank positions are added only where a physical A4 slot is unused. Everything is processed locally in your browser. Everything is processed locally in your browser.

Printing Layout

Arrange complete Pecha PDF pages vertically on printable sheets.

Choose paper size

Pages are placed vertically with 0 mm margin and 0 mm gap. The complete group of pages is centered on every sheet. The downloaded PDF does not rasterize or JPEG-compress the source pages. If a source page is larger than the selected sheet, a warning will ask whether to fit it proportionally to the selected A4, A3 or B4 sheet. A 0.15 mm cutting stroke is added at output positions 1, 51, 101, 151, 201, and so on. All other printing arrangement rules remain unchanged.

Combine Layout

Automatically select 2–6 part Pecha alignment from the selected sheet, then apply the printing layout.

Choose paper size

Combine Layout first calculates how many Pecha pages physically fit vertically on the selected sheet. That number automatically selects the existing 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 part Page Alignment logic. The resulting aligned pages are then arranged using the same Printing Layout placement rules: centered vertically, 0 mm margin, 0 mm gap, proportional fitting when approved, and a 0.15 mm cutting outline on sheets 1, 51, 101, 151, and so on.
